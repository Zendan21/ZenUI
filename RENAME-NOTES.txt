ZENUI RENAME NOTES
==================

ZenUI 0.9.137 is the renamed continuation of RavioliUI 0.9.136.

IMPORTANT INSTALL STEP
----------------------
Remove the old Interface\AddOns\RavioliUI folder before installing ZenUI.
Do not leave both addon folders enabled at the same time.

Your existing RavioliUIDB SavedVariables are loaded once and automatically
migrated into ZenUIDB, including profiles, mover positions and branded scale
keys. Old /rui macros continue to work as silent compatibility aliases, but the
new commands are /zenui and /zen.

New profile exports use ZUI1:. Existing RUI1: profile strings remain importable.
